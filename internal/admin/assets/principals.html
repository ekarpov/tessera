Suspend, reinstate, delete, or reset a password

Suspending stops every credential the account holds at once and keeps them, so reinstating restores exactly what it had — use it for abuse or a lost device. Deleting removes the account and its credentials permanently and retires its name, which is never issued again. Datasets somebody else also administers are kept; a dataset the account is the last administrator of is erased with it, and only when you name it below — deleting without naming it shows you what to enter. Setting a password lets you sign in as the person, so it takes administering every dataset they hold. You may act only on accounts whose every dataset you administer, and not on your own.
